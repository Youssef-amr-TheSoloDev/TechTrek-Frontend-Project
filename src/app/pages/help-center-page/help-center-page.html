Help Center

Find quick answers to the questions readers ask most often about browsing, account access, orders, and book discovery.

How do I search for a book?

Use the search button in the top navigation and enter the title, author, or keyword you want to explore.

Can I browse new arrivals and best sellers?

Yes. The Explore page lets you view featured books, new arrivals, and popular best sellers.

How do I contact support?

You can reach us through the Contact page, by email, or by phone using the details listed in our support section.

Do I need an account to browse books?

No, you can browse and explore titles without an account. An account is helpful for saving preferences and managing your reading journey.

What if I forget my account details?

Use the account recovery options available on the sign-in flow or contact support for help restoring access.
